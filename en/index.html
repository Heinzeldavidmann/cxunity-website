Approach

Good digital solutions rarely come from a single perspective.

Three questions come first: What do people experience? What does the business need? What should still hold true in two years?

Experience perspective

The experience someone has with a digital product determines whether they come back, recommend it, or leave. CXUnity makes sure that experience is right from the start.

Business perspective

A great user experience and strong business goals are not mutually exclusive. More clarity for the user often means more trust, better enquiries and more efficient processes for the business.

Future perspective

Digital solutions should not only be built for today. What works now must still work tomorrow, as the business grows, changes or faces new demands.

FAQ

A few things worth clarifying.

These questions help clarify what CXUnity can support and what working together could look like.

What makes CXUnity different from a typical web agency?

At CXUnity, every project starts with one question: how does this feel for someone who actually uses it? Design and technology come after that. Digital products and services are first experienced firsthand and explored from a user's perspective. What comes up in that process feeds directly into the professional analysis, which draws on an academic background in User Experience and Customer Experience.

What kind of projects can CXUnity support?

CXUnity supports projects around websites, landing pages, digital touchpoints, customer-facing services and smaller web-based solutions. This can include improving an existing website, structuring a new digital presence, clarifying user journeys, reducing friction in digital processes or building a first professional web presence from scratch.

Is CXUnity only relevant for new websites?

No. CXUnity can also support existing websites and digital services. Often the more valuable step is to first understand where users get lost, where information is unclear, where the customer journey breaks down or where the current solution no longer fits the business behind it.

Do I need a fully defined project before reaching out?

No. A first conversation can also start with a vague challenge, an idea or the feeling that a digital touchpoint could work better. Together, we can clarify what the actual problem is, which users or customers are affected and what kind of solution would make sense.

What happens after a website or digital touchpoint goes live?

For CXUnity, launch is not the end of the project. After a digital touchpoint goes live, the important question is whether it actually works in practice. Feedback, user behavior, customer reactions and meaningful metrics can help identify what should be improved next.
