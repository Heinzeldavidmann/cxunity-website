Good digital solutions rarely come from a single perspective.

Three questions come first: What do people experience? What does the business need? What should still hold true in two years?

Experience perspective

The experience someone has with a digital product determines whether they come back, recommend it, or leave. CXUnity makes sure that experience is right from the start.

Business perspective

A great user experience and strong business goals are not mutually exclusive. More clarity for the user often means more trust, better enquiries and more efficient processes for the business.

Future perspective

Digital solutions should not only be built for today. What works now must still work tomorrow, as the business grows, changes or faces new demands.

A few things worth clarifying.

What makes CXUnity different from a typical web agency?

CXUnity starts by asking how your offer feels to the people who use it. Design and technology come after that. CXUnity tests your website or app firsthand and looks at it from your customers' point of view. This builds on an academic background in User Experience and Customer Experience. Your contact stays the same from the first conversation to the result.

What kind of projects can CXUnity support?

Websites, landing pages, apps and smaller web applications. That can be a new website built from scratch, an improvement to an existing site, or a process such as an enquiry or booking that should become easier online.

Is CXUnity only for new websites?

No. Reviewing an existing website is often the more valuable step. Where do visitors drop off, where is information missing, and does the site still fit the business? That is exactly what the experience audit is for.

Do I need a finished project before getting in touch?

No. A conversation can start with an idea or with the feeling that something could work better. Together we clarify what it is really about and which solution makes sense. The initial call is free and non-binding.

What happens after the website launches?

For CXUnity, launch is not the end. That is when it shows whether the site works in everyday use. Enquiries, feedback and figures such as clicks from Google Search show what should improve next.
