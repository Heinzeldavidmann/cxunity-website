How well does your website perform?

Five questions, about one minute. You get a first assessment of where your website is strong and where a closer look is worthwhile.

This is a self-assessment, not an audit. Your answers are neither stored nor transmitted.

1Anyone opening your home page understands within seconds what it is about and what they can do here.
2The navigation offers only a few, clearly named paths, and the most important step stands out right away.
3After an action, such as submitting a form, the site clearly shows that it worked.
4After a typo or a wrong address, people are guided kindly and can easily get back.
5On a phone, the most important button is easy to reach with a thumb, and nothing is too small or too tight.
