3. Hosting and server log files

When this website is accessed, technical data may be processed automatically by the hosting provider. This can include the IP address, date and time of access, requested files, referrer URL, browser type, operating system and the amount of data transferred. 4. Contact by email

If you contact CXUnity by email, the information you provide will be processed for the purpose of handling your request and any follow-up communication. This data will not be passed on without your consent unless there is a legal obligation to do so.

5. Cookies, analytics and external tracking

When you visit this website, a cookie banner asks for your consent before any non-essential cookies are set. If you click "Accept", this website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies stored on your device to help analyse how visitors use the website, such as pages visited, time spent on the site and referring pages.

The information generated is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers that may be located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google may process this data under its own standard contractual clauses with the EU. On this website, IP anonymisation is active, meaning your IP address is shortened by Google beforehand within member states of the EU or the European Economic Area.

This processing takes place solely on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with § 25(1) TTDSG). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of this website, which lets you change or revoke your choice. If you click "Decline" or do not interact with the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded and no such data is collected.

Further information on Google's data processing can be found in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Legal basis

The processing of technical access data is based on the legitimate interest in providing a secure and functional website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The processing of data sent by email is based on the legitimate interest in responding to incoming requests and, where applicable, taking steps prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR). The use of Google Analytics is based exclusively on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TTDSG), which you may withdraw at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request information about personal data processed about you, as well as correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability where legally applicable. You also have the right to object to certain processing activities and to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

8. Updates to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the website, technical setup or legal requirements change.
